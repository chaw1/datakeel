# Synthetic image benchmark sources

These 60 synthetic images comprise 24 bilingual business passages rendered in clean and scanned forms, and 12 structural diagrams. They were generated with Noto Sans SC, licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. The generation scripts and font files remain in the internal provenance archive and are not distributed here. Image bytes are frozen; the original b09 diagram is retained. No research-only handwriting datasets are included.

The cases contain independent gold entities, relations, optional triples, visual traps, and frozen pixel-coordinate evidence boxes. Original recording dates and model identities are included in each recording file. A-group gold boxes were computed once from source layout spans and then frozen. All content is synthetic.
